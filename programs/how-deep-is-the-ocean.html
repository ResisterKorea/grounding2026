How Deep is the Ocean?

DateSunday 11 October

Time16:00—17:00

ArtistErik Peters

Program introduction

“How deep is the ocean?” asks the fish jealous of the whale, her lungs big enough to stretch from the depths to the surface.

Big enough to sustain ecosystems for a hundred years after her final breath. And you? In whatever way you have found a way to breathe through unbreathable conditions. You are still here. There is no end to what a living world will demand of you. You and the whale have so much in common.
