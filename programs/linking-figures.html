Workshop

Linking Figures

DateTuesday 06 October

Time11:00—13:00

ArtistYounwon Sohn

Program introduction

“Touching, posing together, and shaping into sculpture.”

Linking Figures is a small sculpture connecting Amsterdam and Seoul—a figure found in unexpected places, representing linked bodies, rituals, and the spirit of ancient clay figures (토우, to-woo).

Workshop flow

Workshop flow

  1. 01

    Taking Photos

    We will step outside together and use natural sunlight as our light source. In teams of three, two people will create a pose where their bodies touch, while the third person takes photographs—capturing three shots from different angles, including the front and side.

  2. 02

    Sculpting with Clay

    Using clay, we will recreate the connected bodies from the photos, paying special attention to the points of contact where hands or shoulders meet. Then, ask your partner about something they love or hold dear, and craft a miniature version of it out of clay to add to the piece.

  3. 03

    Connecting the Sculptures

    Finally, we will bring everyone’s connected figures together, joining them all into one unified work.
