Program introduction

Myths did not begin as writing. They were carried in voices — sung, called out, whispered from one generation to the next by people who had no other way to keep them. Somewhere along that long transmission the sound fell away, and we were left holding the story alone.

Pansori is one of the places where the sound survived: a single voice, worn by years of training, holding an entire world for hours.

This session moves through three Korean myths, one at a time. For each, I tell the story — then build an original pansori-based song live with a loop station, made in response to what was just told. Between myths, the floor stays open: questions about the myth itself, or about the tradition that still knows how to sing it.

Ask anything. No background in Korean music is needed.
