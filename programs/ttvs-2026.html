Program introduction

This program is based on an online radio show, Time to Take Vitamin Sounds (TTVS), that Mina Kim hosted during 2022–2023. Back then, Mina served as a host every other week on a radio program produced by WORM in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, where she introduced a variety of Korean electronic and experimental music.

This time, Mina will host a live online radio show at Happening, where she plans to showcase the sound work of RE#SISTER Korea members and introduce various Korean electronic music and sounds.
