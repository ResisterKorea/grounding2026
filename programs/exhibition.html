Program introduction

Practices on Contemporary Hydropathy and Orientation is the collaborative sound piece by Irene de Boer and YANNY presented throughout Grounding.

Grounding is a seven-day interdisciplinary project by RE#SISTER Korea, Irene de Boer, and Happening, connecting South Korean artists with the Netherlands through shared research and making.

Happening · Hapjeong, Seoul · 05—11 October 2026
