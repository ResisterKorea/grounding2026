Reading Lounge

Dates05—11 October

FormatOpen reading and gathering space

Program introduction

The Reading Lounge is an open space for reading, rest, and exchange throughout the Grounding program. Books and reference materials—including texts and images—that RE#SISTER Korea and Irene have read, discussed, and shared together will be printed and available in the lounge at Happening. Visitors are welcome to browse the materials, converse with other audiences and participants, and stay for as long as they wish.
