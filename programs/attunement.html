Program introduction

코감응 — at(tune)ment is an experimental olfactory improvisation workshop that explores creating sound in response to smell.

Participants encounter a scent and express the memories, feelings, and bodily responses that arise through a single note or a short musical phrase. Even the same scent may produce very different sounds depending on the distance between the scent and each person, their memories and emotions, and how they feel that day.

There are no predetermined answers in this workshop. Together, we observe and experiment with how a smell passes through different bodies, giving rise to different sounds and relationships.

Let’s Nose-Feel-Yes!

Participants
Up to 5 participants who can play an instrument, plus up to 5 observers.
Applying
Apply as a performing or observing participant. The form asks for your preferred date, name and contact information, any scent allergies or sensitivities, and other notes or requests. Performing participants are also asked for their instrument, power requirements, and any required equipment.
Preparation
Performing participants’ instruments, power requirements, and audio equipment are checked in advance. An individual olfactory attunement scent sample kit and recording sheet are prepared for each participant.
Before the workshop
Participating musicians should arrive before the workshop begins to set up their instruments and equipment.

Workshop flow

Workshop flow

  1. 015 min

    Introduction and brief instructions

  2. 02Steps 02–04 · 20 min

    Attuning to the Sense of Smell

    Encounter a scent and briefly observe not only the immediate sensation but also any memories or bodily responses that arise.

  3. 03

    Responding with a Single Note or Short Phrase

    Express the sensory experience with a single note or short phrase using your instrument.

  4. 04

    Repeat Approximately Three Times

    Repeat the process several times with different scents or olfactory situations, exploring how you respond to smell through sound.

  5. 0515–20 min

    Olfactory Response Improvisation

    Afterward, engage in a free improvisation, responding to changes in the scent and the sounds of other musicians.

  6. 0610 min

    Brief sharing and closing
